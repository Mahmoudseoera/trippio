ZOOM INTO THE STORY

Places to discover

Walk beside the Great Pyramids of Giza, then meet the Great Sphinx on the edge of the desert. These remarkable monuments are the starting point of your journey into ancient Egypt.

The Great Sphinx watches over the Giza plateau with the body of a lion and a human head. Discover this extraordinary monument alongside the pyramids.

Explore the Grand Egyptian Museum near the Giza pyramids, where ancient Egyptian collections are presented in a remarkable modern setting.

Visit the Egyptian Museum in Tahrir Square, an iconic home for ancient Egyptian antiquities in the heart of Cairo.

Wander the lively lanes of Khan el-Khalili, where traditional shops, metalwork, spices and old cafés reveal a different side of Cairo.

Travel to the island sanctuary of Philae. Dedicated to the goddess Isis, this temple complex was moved to protect it from rising waters after the Aswan High Dam was built.

Kom Ombo is an unusual double temple beside the Nile. Its paired sanctuaries honour Sobek, the crocodile god, and Horus the Elder.

At Edfu, step into one of Egypt’s best-preserved ancient temples. Its monumental gateways and carved walls tell stories of the falcon god Horus.

On the west bank of Luxor, the Valley of the Kings holds royal tombs cut deep into the desert hills. Explore the burial ground of Egypt’s New Kingdom pharaohs.

The terraced temple of Hatshepsut rises against the cliffs of Deir el-Bahari. Discover the architecture and story of one of ancient Egypt’s remarkable rulers.

Explore Karnak’s vast temple complex and the towering columns of its Great Hypostyle Hall. Generations of rulers expanded this sacred landscape on the east bank of the Nile.

Luxor Temple stands in the heart of the modern city. Discover its monumental courtyards and its connection to Karnak through the Avenue of Sphinxes.

After your days exploring temples and tombs, unwind by Hurghada’s Red Sea coast. Enjoy the beach, clear water and time to relax before your departure.
