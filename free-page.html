Terms & Conditions

Terms & Conditions

By booking any tour, excursion, package, or service with Brand Name ("we", "our", "the Company"), you ("the Client") agree to be bound by the following terms and conditions. Please read them carefully before confirming any reservation.

1. Booking & Confirmation

A booking is confirmed only when Brand Name sends you a written confirmation email. No verbal commitments constitute a binding contract. A deposit of 20% of the total tour price is required to secure your booking. The remaining balance must be paid no later than 7 days before the tour departure date.

2. Pricing

All prices quoted are in USD unless stated otherwise. Prices include all listed services and applicable taxes at the time of quoting. Brand Name reserves the right to adjust prices if costs change significantly due to fuel surcharges, government taxes, or currency fluctuations — you will be notified before any change affects your confirmed booking.

3. Client Responsibilities

  • Clients must provide accurate personal information including passport details, nationality, and emergency contacts.
  • It is the client's responsibility to ensure they hold a valid passport (minimum 6 months validity BrandName travel date) and any required visas.
  • Clients are expected to be at departure points on time. Brand Name bears no liability for clients who miss departures due to lateness.
  • Clients must behave respectfully toward local customs, heritage sites, and fellow travelers.

4. Health & Special Requirements

Clients with medical conditions, disabilities, or dietary requirements must notify us in writing at the time of booking. We will make every reasonable effort to accommodate needs, but cannot guarantee all requests depending on destination constraints.

5. Liability

Brand Name acts as an organiser of travel services provided by independent third parties (hotels, transport operators, guides). While we take every care in selecting partners, we cannot accept liability for injury, loss, damage, delay, or expense arising from acts outside our direct control, including force majeure events (natural disasters, pandemics, political instability, or government restrictions).

These Terms are governed by Egyptian law. Any disputes will be first addressed through mediation; if unresolved, through the courts of Cairo, Egypt.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellation Policy

We understand that travel plans sometimes change. Our cancellation policy is designed to be fair to both clients and our local partners who commit resources in advance.

Client-Initiated Cancellations

Cancellations must be submitted in writing to info@egyptgotravel.com. The date we receive your written notice determines which fee applies.

Days Before Departure Refund Entitlement Cancellation Fee
30+ days 100% refund No charge
15–29 days 75% refund 25% of tour price
8–14 days 50% refund 50% of tour price
3–7 days 25% refund 75% of tour price
0–2 days / No-show No refund 100% of tour price

Non-refundable components — such as domestic flights, entry permits (Abu Simbel, Valley of the Kings), and prepaid accommodation blocks — may not be refundable regardless of the cancellation date. These will be clearly marked at booking.

Company-Initiated Cancellations

In the rare event that Brand Name must cancel your tour (due to insufficient bookings, force majeure, or safety concerns), you will receive:

  • A full 100% refund of all payments made, or
  • A credit voucher of equal value valid for 24 months, or
  • An alternative tour of equivalent or greater value at no extra cost.
Refund Process

Refund Process

Once a cancellation is confirmed in writing and eligibility is verified, refunds are processed as follows:

How Refunds Are Issued

  • Refunds are returned via the same payment method used for the original booking (credit card, bank transfer, or PayPal).
  • Credit card refunds typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your card issuer.
  • Bank transfer refunds are processed within 7–14 business days.
  • PayPal refunds are usually returned within 3–5 business days.

Refund Deductions

The following may be deducted from any refund:

  • Bank transfer fees or international transaction charges incurred during the original payment.
  • Currency conversion losses if the original payment was made in a currency other than USD.
  • Non-refundable third-party fees explicitly disclosed at booking (certain permits, flights, etc.).

To initiate a refund, email info@egyptgotravel.com with your booking reference number and the subject line "Cancellation Request". We aim to acknowledge all requests within 24 hours.

Date Changes

Changing Your Travel Dates

We understand life happens. Date change requests are handled on a case-by-case basis, subject to availability.

  • Date changes requested 14+ days before departure are free of administrative charge (though any price difference applies).
  • Date changes requested 7–13 days before departure incur a $25 per person administration fee.
  • Date changes within 7 days of departure are treated as a cancellation and re-booking, and the standard cancellation fees apply.
  • Maximum of 2 date changes per booking. Subsequent changes are treated as cancellations.
Travel Insurance

Travel Insurance

Brand Name strongly recommends that all clients purchase comprehensive travel insurance before departure. A good policy should cover:

  • Trip cancellation and curtailment due to illness, injury, or unforeseen events
  • Medical expenses and emergency evacuation while abroad
  • Baggage loss or delay
  • Personal liability
  • Trip disruption due to public health emergencies (where available)

We do not sell insurance directly but can recommend reputable providers. Failure to purchase travel insurance does not reduce or waive any cancellation fees that may apply.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Brand Name is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

What We Collect

  • Name, email address, phone number, nationality, and passport details (for booking purposes)
  • Payment information (processed securely; we do not store full card numbers)
  • Communication records (emails, WhatsApp messages)
  • Website usage data (anonymised analytics via cookies)

How We Use Your Data

  • To process and manage your bookings
  • To communicate tour updates, changes, or relevant offers
  • To comply with legal obligations (visa processing, border authorities)
  • To improve our services through aggregated analytics

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data at any time. Contact us at privacy@egyptgotravel.com. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.
