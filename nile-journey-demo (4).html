Overview

This tour follows the country's oldest road, the Nile, from the sea in the north to the temples of Nubia in the south. You will see the Pyramids, Karnak and Abu Simbel without queuing for transport, with a private driver and a flight to save a full day on the road.

Each day has a morning start for the cooler hours and a free afternoon, so you can add a market visit, a boat ride or simply rest. Hotels are chosen for location and a quiet night.

Highlights

  • Walk the Alexandria Corniche and Qaitbay Citadel
  • Stand at the foot of the Great Pyramid and the Sphinx
  • Explore the Grand Egyptian Museum with an Egyptologist
  • See Karnak Temple and the Valley of the Kings
  • Sail a felucca around Elephantine Island in Aswan
  • Watch the sun light the colossi of Abu Simbel

Frequently asked questions

Is this a private tour?

Yes. The tour is private, for 1 to 12 people, with your own air-conditioned vehicle, driver and licensed Egyptologist guide.

Which languages do the guides speak?

English and Arabic.

How do we travel between the cities?

By private vehicle, except Cairo to Luxor, which is a domestic flight included in the price.

What is not included?

International flights, the Egypt visa fee, lunches and dinners, tips for the guide and driver, the optional hot-air balloon in Luxor, and travel insurance and personal expenses.

How is the price calculated?

Prices are per person in US dollars and depend on group size and hotel class (3, 4 or 5-star). The more travelers share the vehicle and guide, the lower the price per person. Children under 6 travel free when sharing a room.

Can I add a market visit or a boat ride?

Yes. Each day has a morning start and a free afternoon, so you can add a market visit, a boat ride or simply rest.
