Day 1 · Cairo

Arrival in Cairo

Land in Cairo and meet your guide for transfer to your hotel, followed by an evening Nile dinner cruise with a live Tanoura show.

Day 2 · Giza

Cairo — Pyramids, Sphinx & Grand Egyptian Museum

Visit the Great Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx, then explore the treasures of the Grand Egyptian Museum, including Tutankhamun's collection.

Day 3 · Cairo

Cairo to Aswan — Egyptian Museum & Khan el-Khalili

Browse the spice-scented alleys of Khan el-Khalili bazaar before your domestic flight to Aswan to join your Nile cruise ship.

Day 4 · Aswan

Aswan — Nile Cruise Embarkation & Philae Temple

Board your Nile cruise ship and visit the beautiful island Temple of Philae, dedicated to the goddess Isis.

Day 5 · Edfu

Nile Sailing — Kom Ombo & Edfu Temples

Sail toward Luxor with stops at the unique double temple of Kom Ombo and the well-preserved Temple of Horus at Edfu.

Day 6 · Luxor

Luxor — West Bank: Valley of the Kings & Hatshepsut Temple

Descend into the royal tombs of the Valley of the Kings and admire the terraced Temple of Hatshepsut carved into the cliffside.

Day 7 · Luxor

Luxor to Hurghada — Karnak & Luxor Temples

Walk the towering columns of Karnak Temple and stroll through Luxor Temple before transferring to the Red Sea resort town of Hurghada.

Day 8 · Hurghada

Hurghada — Red Sea Relaxation & Departure

Enjoy a full day of relaxation on Hurghada's beautiful beaches, with sunshine, crystal-clear waters, and optional water sports, before your transfer to Hurghada International Airport for departure.

Before you go

A few simple things to sort out before your tour begins, so everything on the road feels easy.

Pack with confidence

Six points to check before you fly

Egypt tour

Passport and visa

Your passport should be valid for at least six months after arrival. Check whether you need a visa and keep a digital copy.

Light, modest clothing

Breathable fabrics that cover shoulders and knees work best for mosques, churches and local streets.

Comfortable walking shoes

Temples and tombs mean uneven stone and sand. Closed, broken-in shoes will save your feet.

Sun protection and water

Bring a hat, sunglasses and sunscreen. Your guide will keep bottled water on the vehicle.

Small cash for tips

Carry small Egyptian pound notes for tips and little purchases at markets and rest stops.

Camera and power adapter

Egypt uses Type C and F sockets at 220V. Bring a spare battery and extra storage for photos.
