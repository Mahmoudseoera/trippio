Trip Overview

Embark on an unforgettable 8-day Egypt Classic Tour featuring the ultimate Nile River cruise experience. Discover ancient wonders from Cairo and Aswan and Luxor, then unwind on Hurghada's Red Sea beaches.

This comprehensive journey combines iconic historical sites with luxurious relaxation. Your adventure includes a magical Nile River cruise between Aswan and Luxor, sailing past timeless landscapes.

Day 1 · Cairo

Arrival in Cairo

Land in Cairo and meet your guide for transfer to your hotel, followed by an evening Nile dinner cruise with a live Tanoura show.

Day 2 · Giza

Cairo — Pyramids, Sphinx & Grand Egyptian Museum

Visit the Great Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx, then explore the treasures of the Grand Egyptian Museum, including Tutankhamun's collection.

Day 3 · Cairo

Cairo to Aswan — Egyptian Museum & Khan el-Khalili

Browse the spice-scented alleys of Khan el-Khalili bazaar before your domestic flight to Aswan to join your Nile cruise ship.

Day 4 · Aswan

Aswan — Nile Cruise Embarkation & Philae Temple

Board your Nile cruise ship and visit the beautiful island Temple of Philae, dedicated to the goddess Isis.

Day 5 · Edfu

Nile Sailing — Kom Ombo & Edfu Temples

Sail toward Luxor with stops at the unique double temple of Kom Ombo and the well-preserved Temple of Horus at Edfu.

Day 6 · Luxor

Luxor — West Bank: Valley of the Kings & Hatshepsut Temple

Descend into the royal tombs of the Valley of the Kings and admire the terraced Temple of Hatshepsut carved into the cliffside.

Day 7 · Luxor

Luxor to Hurghada — Karnak & Luxor Temples

Walk the towering columns of Karnak Temple and stroll through Luxor Temple before transferring to the Red Sea resort town of Hurghada.

Day 8 · Hurghada

Hurghada — Red Sea Relaxation & Departure

Enjoy a full day of relaxation on Hurghada's beautiful beaches, with sunshine, crystal-clear waters, and optional water sports, before your transfer to Hurghada International Airport for departure.

Frequently asked questions

Are dietary restrictions accommodated during meals on this Egypt Classic Tour?

Yes — let us know about any allergies or dietary needs (vegetarian, vegan, halal, gluten-free) when booking, and both the hotels and the cruise kitchen will accommodate them.

How do I book the Egypt Classic Tour and is instant confirmation available?

Select your travel dates and traveler count in the booking form, and you'll receive instant confirmation by email once payment is complete.

What should I pack for 8 days in Egypt including Cairo, Nile cruise, and Hurghada?

Lightweight breathable clothing, a hat and sunscreen, modest coverage for temple visits, swimwear for Hurghada, and comfortable walking shoes.

Will I have free time to explore on my own during the tour?

Yes, evenings on the cruise and the Hurghada day are left open for you to relax, explore, or book optional activities.

Where and when will you pick me up in Cairo?

Our representative meets you at Cairo International Airport arrivals hall holding a sign with your name, right after you clear customs.
